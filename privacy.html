Privacy Policy

Applies to all use of VALLENT EXS across Discord servers.

01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VALLENT EXS ("the bot", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when it is added to and used within a Discord server. By inviting VALLENT EXS to a server or interacting with its commands, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

VALLENT EXS operates within the Discord platform and is bound by Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service in addition to this document.

02

Information We Collect

To operate its moderation and server management features, VALLENT EXS collects and processes the following limited categories of data:

  • Discord User ID — used to identify members for moderation actions, permission checks, and command execution.
  • Server ID (Guild ID) — used to store server-specific configuration, such as moderation settings and feature preferences.
  • Channel ID — used to route logs, tickets, and automated actions to the correct channel.
  • Role ID — used to manage permission levels, premium access, and role-based automation.
  • Command usage data — records of which commands are executed, when, and by whom, used for tracking, rate-limiting, and diagnostics.
03

How We Use Information

All information listed above is collected strictly to operate the features of VALLENT EXS. This includes:

  • Executing moderation actions such as warnings, mutes, kicks, and bans.
  • Maintaining per-server configuration, including logging channels and ticket categories.
  • Enforcing role-based permissions and premium feature access.
  • Diagnosing issues, preventing abuse, and improving reliability.

Data is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the operation of the bot.

04

Information We Do Not Collect

VALLENT EXS does not read, log, or store the contents of personal conversations. Message content is only processed transiently, in memory, when strictly required to perform an active moderation check (for example, scanning a message for a prohibited link at the moment it is sent), and is not retained afterward.

We do not collect passwords, payment card details, private messages, or any information beyond what is described in this policy.

05

Data Sharing

Data collected by VALLENT EXS is never sold, rented, or traded to any third party. Information may only be disclosed in the following limited circumstances:

  • When required to comply with a valid legal request or applicable law.
  • When necessary to investigate or prevent abuse, security threats, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • With infrastructure providers strictly necessary to host and operate the bot (for example, database and hosting services), bound to appropriate confidentiality standards.
06

Data Retention & Removal

Data is retained for as long as VALLENT EXS remains active in a server and as needed to provide its features. If the bot is removed from a server, associated configuration data is eventually deleted from active systems.

Server administrators or individual users may request the removal of data associated with their server or account, where technically feasible and where retention is not required for security, legal, or anti-abuse purposes. Removal requests can be submitted through the contact method listed below.

07

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect stored data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. Access to stored data is restricted to what is necessary for operating and maintaining VALLENT EXS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08

Children's Privacy

VALLENT EXS is intended for use in accordance with Discord's own minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect data beyond the identifiers described in this policy from any user, and access to the bot is governed by Discord's platform-level age restrictions.

09

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in functionality, legal requirements, or operational practices. Continued use of VALLENT EXS after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy, or requests related to data removal, can be directed to the development team responsible for VALLENT EXS.
